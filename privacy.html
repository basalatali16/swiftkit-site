Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026
✅ SwiftKit extensions do NOT collect, store, or sell any personal data. All processing happens locally in your browser.

1. Who We Are

SwiftKit ("we", "us", "our") builds Chrome browser extensions. Our products include SlotSniper and SwiftGrab. We are operated by an individual developer.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

Our extensions do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or any personal information
  • Your browsing history or visited websites
  • Your location or IP address
  • Any usage analytics or telemetry
  • Payment details (handled entirely by Paddle)

3. Data Stored Locally On Your Device

Our extensions store the following data ONLY on your local device using Chrome's built-in storage. This data never leaves your device:

  • URLs you add to monitor (SlotSniper)
  • Your tracker settings and preferences
  • Activity logs of checks performed
  • Your Pro subscription status (license key)
  • WhatsApp number and API key you provide (stored locally only)

4. Network Requests

SlotSniper makes HTTP requests ONLY to the appointment booking URLs that you explicitly add. These go directly from your browser to those websites — no data passes through SwiftKit servers. We have no servers that receive your data.

SwiftGrab makes requests to URLs you choose to download. Same principle applies.

5. WhatsApp Notifications

If you enable WhatsApp alerts (Pro feature), your phone number and API key are sent directly to CallMeBot's servers (callmebot.com) to deliver notifications. We do not store or see this data. Please review CallMeBot's privacy policy at callmebot.com.

6. Payments

Payments are processed by Paddle (paddle.com). We never see or store your payment card details. Paddle handles all billing, taxes, and receipts. When you purchase, Paddle may share your email with us to deliver your license key. Please review Paddle's privacy policy at paddle.com/legal/privacy.

7. Chrome Permissions Explained

  • alarms — Check appointment pages at set intervals
  • storage — Save your settings locally on your device
  • notifications — Show alerts when slots are found
  • tabs — Open booking page when slot found
  • activeTab / scripting — Show status banner on appointment pages
  • host_permissions — Access appointment pages you add to monitor

8. Children's Privacy

Our extensions are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. The date at the top reflects the latest update. Continued use means you accept any changes.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy: privacy@swiftkit.app
